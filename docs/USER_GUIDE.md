# AKILIMO Mobile — User Guide

> Checked against the app on 2026-11-17.

---

## Getting Started

### First launch

The first time you open AKILIMO you go through a one-time setup. Each step collects what the app needs to give advice for your farm. You do this once; afterwards the app opens on the home screen.

1. **Welcome** — Choose your language: English, Kiswahili or Kinyarwanda. The app changes language straight away.
2. **Disclaimer** — Read and accept the agronomic disclaimer.
3. **Terms** — Read and accept the terms of use.
4. **About you** — Your first and last name and gender (optional), why you are interested in AKILIMO, and the language you want your advice in.
5. **Country** — Nigeria, Tanzania, Ghana, Rwanda or Burundi.
6. **Location** — Allow GPS, or pick your farm on the map. The app tells you if your farm is outside the area where AKILIMO can give advice.
7. **Area unit** — Acre, Hectare or Are.
8. **Planting dates** — Your planting date and expected harvest date. The harvest must be between 235 and 452 days after planting.
9. **Tillage** — The tillage operations you use.
10. **Investment** — How often you invest in new farm products.
11. **Summary** — Review your answers and finish.

### The home screen

After setup the app opens on the home screen:

- **Recommendations** — get advice for your farm.
- **Profile** — change your personal details.
- **Your farm** — change your country and location, field size and investment preference.
- **Settings** — language, appearance and more.
- **Account** — sign in or create an account (optional, see below).

## Main Flows

### Getting a recommendation

1. On the home screen tap **Recommendations**.
2. Choose the advice you want:
   - **Fertilizer recommendation** (cassava, maize, sweet potato)
   - **Best planting practices**
   - **Scheduled planting and high starch**
   - **Intercropping with maize** or **with sweet potato**
3. Complete the steps shown. A step is marked done when you have saved it. Steps can include:
   - **Fertilizers** — choose the fertilizers you can buy, tap each one and pick a price (or "I don't know", or enter the exact price)
   - **Investment amount**
   - **Planting and harvest dates** — optional flexibility of one or two months
   - **Cassava yield**, **market outlet** (starch factory or regular market, with the unit you sell in and your price)
   - **Maize or sweet potato market** and **maize performance**
   - **Tillage costs**, **tractor access** and **weed control cost**
4. Tap **Get recommendations** and read the advice.
5. Optionally tell us how useful the advice was.

### Reading the advice comfortably

- Use the text size control to make the advice text larger.
- Switch the screen to landscape to see more of the text at once; the app remembers your choice.

### Refreshing prices and options

The fertilizer, cassava market and investment screens show options that the app downloads. If they look out of date, **pull down** on the list to refresh. You need an internet connection.

### Changing your language

1. Home screen → **Settings** → **Language**, and choose your language. It changes straight away.
2. Turn on **Lock app language** if you want the app to keep this language even when the phone's language is different.

You can also change the language you receive advice in under **Profile**.

### Changing your details

- **Profile** — your name, gender, interest and recommendation language.
- **Your farm** — your country and location, field size and investment preference.
- Planting and harvest dates, tillage and weed control are changed in their own steps under **Recommendations**.

### Your account (optional)

You do not need an account to get advice. With an account you can keep your advice and open it again later, even on a new phone.

1. On the home screen tap **Create account**, or **Sign in** if you already have one. You can use your email and a password, or sign in with Google.
2. Confirm your email with the code we send you. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password** to get a code and set a new one.
3. Agree to the consent text. Your advice is saved to your account only after you do.
4. Open **Saved advice** to see your list. You can swipe right to delete, swipe left to share, select several to delete them together, or delete everything. You can also delete your account.

## Feature Reference

### Advice types

| Advice | Crops | What it gives you |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Fertilizer recommendation | Cassava, maize, sweet potato | Fertilizer type and amount for your farm |
| Intercropping | Cassava with maize, cassava with sweet potato | Fertilizer plan for the two crops together |
| Scheduled planting (high starch) | Cassava | Best timing and fertilizer for high starch |
| Best planting practices | Cassava | Planting practices for your field |

### Inputs

| Input | Where |
|-------|-------|
| Fertilizers and their prices | Recommendations → Fertilizers |
| Investment amount | Recommendations → Investment |
| Planting and harvest dates | Recommendations → Dates |
| Cassava yield | Recommendations → Yield |
| Cassava, maize and sweet potato market and price | Recommendations → the crop's market step |
| Tillage, manual tillage cost, tractor access | Recommendations → Tillage steps |
| Weed control cost | Recommendations → Weed control |

### Settings

- **Language**, and **Lock app language**
- **Theme** (follow the phone, light or dark) and **Text size** for reading advice
- **Fertilizer list** as list or grid, and **remember area unit**
- **Farm location** and the coverage area map
- **Data and storage** — clear saved recommendations
- **About** — app version, privacy policy and terms

## Known Limitations

| Limitation | What to do |
|------------|-----------|
| Advice needs an internet connection | Connect before tapping **Get recommendations**; downloaded prices and options work offline |
| Saved advice needs an account | Create an account if you want your advice kept; without one, note the results before leaving the screen |
| The harvest date must be 235 to 452 days after planting | Pick a planting date that fits your harvest plan |
| Notifications by email or SMS are not available | — |
